Privacy Policy — One Dad Joke

Last updated: September 14, 2026

One Dad Joke does not collect any data. Full stop.

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • The app makes no network connections. All jokes are bundled in the app and shown entirely on your device.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app stores small preferences on your device (which joke you're on and your chosen Dad-ness level). This never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing to share, sell, or delete on a server — there is no server.

Children's privacy

One Dad Joke is safe for children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. It complies with children's privacy laws (e.g. COPPA/GDPR-K) by simply not collecting, using, or disclosing any personal information.
